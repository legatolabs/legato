# Mac app personas (Legato for Mac: menu bar, say or type a task)

The people most likely to use it, most important first.

## 1. Technical prosumer / early adopter
- **Who:** a developer or tech-savvy person who tries every AI tool, and already has an OpenRouter, OpenAI or Anthropic key.
- **Wants:** a faster "Claude computer use" on their own Mac, with their own model and key, and no code.
- **Cares about:** speed, cost (the per-task cap), and it staying out of the way (background mode).

## 2. Founder / operator with repetitive desktop work
- **Who:** runs a small team and spends hours in browser admin panels, CRMs, spreadsheets and email.
- **Wants:** "fill these 20 rows into the CRM" said once (hold Fn) while they do something else.
- **Cares about:** that it finishes correctly, that it asks before risky actions, and what each task cost.
- **Gap:** reliability on long, real-world tasks; apps beyond Safari, Chrome and TextEdit in background mode.

## 3. Privacy-first local-model user
- **Who:** runs a model on their own Mac or GPU (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM) and doesn't want screens sent to the cloud.
- **Wants:** the app pointed at `localhost`, so nothing leaves the machine.
- **Gap:** small local models need to be good enough; recommend which ones.

## 4. Accessibility / voice-first user
- **Who:** finds the mouse and keyboard slow or hard, and prefers to say what to do.
- **Wants:** to hold Fn, say the task, and watch it happen.
- **Cares about:** voice accuracy, and clear steps they can follow and stop.
